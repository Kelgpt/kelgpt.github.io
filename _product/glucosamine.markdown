<p>TAURUS Premium Glucosamine Plus Joint Support contains active ingredients that have a beneficial effect on your pet's joint health and mobility:</p>
<br>
<p>Glucosamine helps rebuild and repair damaged cartilage, reduces joint inflammation, improves joint lubrication, and slows down cartilage degeneration, keeping your pet's joints flexible and pain-free.</p>
<br>
<p>Chondroitin works synergistically with glucosamine to maintain cartilage elasticity, prevent cartilage breakdown, reduce joint pain and stiffness, and improve overall joint function and flexibility.</p>
<br>
<p>MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane) provides natural anti-inflammatory support, reduces joint swelling and discomfort, promotes collagen production for healthy connective tissue, and enhances nutrient absorption for optimal joint health.
TAURUS Premium Glucosamine Plus Joint Support helps to maintain your pet's mobility and joint comfort, allowing them to stay active, playful, and agile throughout their life.</p>
