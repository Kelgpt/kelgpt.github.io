<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

<p>With so many Yonex badminton rackets on the market how do you know which is the best one to go for?
<br>
Well largely this depends upon 2 main aspects:
<br>
1. Badminton discipline you play - Singles, Doubles or Mixed Doubles?
<br>
2. Personal playing style - Attacking (with lots of smash & power play), Defensive & Speed (counter attacking and out manoeuvring your opponents) or All-Round (an even mixture of attacking and defensive play).
<br>
If you're needing further advice other than what's below on how to choose - check out our How to Choose a Badminton Racket Guide.</p>
